# Product Requirements

## Vision
Make cold-call practice as repeatable as a gym workout: realistic voice reps, immediate evidence-based coaching, measurable progress.

## Primary users
- SDRs / BDRs
- Account executives
- founders
- real-estate agents
- recruiters
- insurance sales reps

## MVP journey
1. Sign up
2. Choose scenario
3. Choose/randomize prospect
4. Start call
5. Speak naturally with AI prospect
6. End call or hit limit
7. Get coaching
8. Practice again or buy credits

## Starter scenarios
- Busy executive
- "Send me an email"
- "Not interested"
- "We already use a competitor"
- Gatekeeper
- Price objection

## Freemium
- 10 free voice minutes/day
- then paid credits
- 1 credit = 1 extra voice minute

## Non-goals for MVP
- real outbound dialing
- CRM integrations
- enterprise SSO
- calling real people
